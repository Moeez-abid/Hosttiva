# Executive Summary

Hosttiva by Inventomo is specified as a modern hosting and cloud platform combining web hosting, domains, business email, cloud/VPS infrastructure, application deployment, AI and automation services, reseller tools, game hosting, marketplace services, and managed technical support. Inventomo provides the development, design, AI integration, automation, migration, and custom-build expertise behind the infrastructure.

The master specification below consolidates the supplied product architecture, website content, client-area requirements, plans, marketplace, resources, legal/launch requirements, SEO and AI discoverability requirements, security, trust, developer, community, and future roadmap sections.
