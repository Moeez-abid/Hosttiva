## 1. Report abuse

Email **[abuse@hosttiva.com]** with:

- the URL, IP address or email headers involved;
- what the problem is (spam, phishing, malware, attack, illegal content);
- when you saw it;
- evidence such as screenshots or full message headers.

We aim to acknowledge reports within **[24]** hours.

## 2. Copyright infringement

Send a notice to **[copyright contact]** including:

- the work you believe is infringed, and proof that you own it or act for the owner;
- the exact URL of the infringing material;
- your contact details;
- a statement that the information is accurate and you are authorised to act.

We will pass the notice to our customer and act according to applicable law.

## 3. Counter-notices

Customers who believe content was removed by mistake can reply with a counter-notice to the same address.

## 4. What happens next

We investigate, contact our customer where appropriate, and act under the [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use). We cannot always share the outcome for privacy reasons.
