## 1. How to report

Email **security@hosttiva.com** with steps to reproduce, the affected URL or service, and the impact. Our machine-readable contact is at [/.well-known/security.txt](/.well-known/security.txt).

## 2. Scope

**In scope:** hosttiva.com, client.hosttiva.com, status.hosttiva.com, and services we operate.
**Out of scope:** customer websites hosted on our servers (report to the site owner, or via [abuse reporting](/legal/abuse)), and third-party services.

## 3. Please do not

- access, change or delete data that is not yours;
- run denial-of-service tests or automated scans that degrade service;
- use social engineering or physical attacks;
- disclose the issue publicly before we have fixed it or **[90]** days have passed.

## 4. Our commitment

We will acknowledge your report within **[3]** business days, keep you updated, and credit you when we publish a fix if you wish. **[Bug bounty: to be decided.]**
