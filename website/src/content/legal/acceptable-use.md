## 1. Purpose

This policy protects our network, our customers and the wider internet. It applies to every service and to anyone using a service you control.

## 2. Not allowed

- Illegal content or activity under the laws of **[Pakistan]** or the place the content is served to.
- Content that infringes copyright, trademarks or other rights.
- Malware, phishing, botnets, or hosting tools to attack other systems.
- Unsolicited bulk email or messages (spam), or buying and using email lists. See the [Email Usage Policy](/legal/email).
- Port scanning, brute-force attempts or denial-of-service attacks against any system.
- Cryptocurrency mining on shared hosting **[and on VPS plans unless agreed in writing]**.
- Child sexual abuse material. We report it to the authorities and terminate the account immediately.
- **[Other categories to confirm: adult content, gambling, file sharing, proxies/VPN exits.]**

## 3. Fair use of resources

Shared hosting accounts must not use resources in a way that degrades service for others. If an account consistently exceeds its plan, we will contact you to optimise or upgrade before taking action, except in emergencies.

## 4. Security responsibilities

Keep your software updated, use strong passwords, and fix compromised sites promptly. We may isolate a compromised account to protect others.

## 5. Enforcement

Depending on severity, we may warn, limit, suspend or terminate a service. We will normally contact you first, but may act immediately where there is serious harm or legal risk.

See [Abuse and Copyright Reporting](/legal/abuse).
