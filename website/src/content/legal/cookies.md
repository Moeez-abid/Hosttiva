## 1. What this covers

This policy explains the cookies and local storage used on hosttiva.com and in the client area.

## 2. What we use today

| Name | Where | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| `theme` (local storage) | hosttiva.com | Remembers light or dark mode | Until cleared |
| Client area session cookie | client.hosttiva.com | Keeps you signed in | Session |
| **[Payment provider cookies]** | Checkout | Fraud prevention | **[Per provider]** |

Our website analytics (**[Cloudflare Web Analytics]**) do not set cookies.

## 3. Optional cookies

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. If we add analytics that use cookies (for example Google Analytics or Microsoft Clarity), we will ask for your consent first and update this table.

## 4. Managing cookies

You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking the client area session cookie will prevent you from signing in.
