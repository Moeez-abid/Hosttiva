## 1. Registry rules

Domains are registered through **[registrar / reseller name]**. Each extension has its own registry rules, which you agree to when you register it.

## 2. Accurate contact details

ICANN and registries require accurate registrant details. Domains with false or outdated details can be suspended. You must respond to verification emails within **[15]** days.

## 3. Renewals and expiry

- Domains renew automatically if auto-renew is on and payment succeeds.
- After expiry there is a grace period of **[X]** days, then a redemption period with a restore fee of **[amount]**, as set by the registry.
- We are not responsible for domains lost because renewal was turned off or payment failed.

## 4. Transfers

- Transfers in require an authorisation (EPP) code and an unlocked domain.
- Transfers out are possible at any time except within **[60]** days of registration or a previous transfer, where registry rules prevent it.

## 5. Privacy

WHOIS privacy is available for extensions that support it **[at no charge / for a fee]**.

## 6. Disputes

Domain disputes follow the UDRP or the registry's dispute policy. We follow lawful orders from registries and courts.
