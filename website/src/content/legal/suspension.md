## 1. Reasons for suspension

- Unpaid invoices.
- Breach of the [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use) or [Email Usage Policy](/legal/email).
- A compromised account that is harming other customers or third parties.
- A lawful request from an authority.

## 2. Notice

For unpaid invoices we send reminders before suspending, at **[X]** and **[Y]** days overdue. For abuse or security issues we may suspend immediately and notify you as soon as possible.

## 3. Restoring a service

Pay the outstanding invoice or resolve the issue, then contact support. Restoration may carry a fee of **[amount]** **[to confirm]**.

## 4. Termination

Services suspended for non-payment are terminated after **[Y]** days. Termination deletes the data on the service; backups are kept only as described in the [Backup Policy](/legal/backup).

## 5. Closing your own account

You can close your account at any time from the client area. Download your data first.
