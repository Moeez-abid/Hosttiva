## 1. Commitment

**[Uptime target to confirm per service, for example 99.9% monthly for shared hosting and network.]** Uptime is measured by our independent monitoring at status.hosttiva.com.

## 2. What counts as downtime

Time when the service is unreachable because of a fault in our infrastructure or network, confirmed by our monitoring or by a support ticket.

## 3. What does not count

- Scheduled maintenance announced at least **[48]** hours in advance.
- Problems caused by your code, configuration, or third-party services.
- Suspension under our policies.
- Events outside our reasonable control.

## 4. Service credits

| Monthly uptime | Credit |
|---|---|
| **[< 99.9%]** | **[5%]** of that month's fee |
| **[< 99.5%]** | **[10%]** |
| **[< 99.0%]** | **[25%]** |

Credits are applied to future invoices and are capped at **[100%]** of the monthly fee for the affected service.

## 5. Claiming

Open a ticket within **[30]** days of the incident, with the service and the times affected.
