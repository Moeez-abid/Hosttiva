## 1. Permission-based email only

You must have permission from everyone you send marketing email to. Purchased, rented or scraped lists are not allowed.

## 2. Every marketing email must

- Include a working unsubscribe link, honoured within **[10]** days.
- Show accurate sender details and a physical address.
- Use a subject line that reflects the content.

## 3. Sending limits

Shared hosting mailboxes are limited to **[X]** messages per hour and **[Y]** per day. Use a dedicated email marketing or transactional service for higher volumes.

## 4. Bounces and complaints

High bounce or complaint rates (above **[X%]**) harm deliverability for all customers. We may pause sending until lists are cleaned.

## 5. Authentication

We recommend SPF, DKIM and DMARC on every sending domain. See our [knowledge base guide](/knowledgebase/deliverability-records).

## 6. Enforcement

Accounts that send spam may be limited or suspended under the [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use).
