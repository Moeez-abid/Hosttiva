## 1. Licences

You must hold any game licences required to run a server, and follow each game's EULA (for example, rules about selling in-game advantages).

## 2. Mods and plugins

Use mods and plugins from trusted sources. You are responsible for their behaviour and for keeping them updated.

## 3. Resources

Game plans include the RAM and CPU allocation listed on the plan. Running non-game software, mining, or proxying traffic on a game server is not allowed.

## 4. Community conduct

You are responsible for content and behaviour on your server, including player chat, and must act on lawful complaints.

## 5. DDoS and attacks

**[Describe DDoS protection once confirmed.]** Servers that attract repeated attacks may be moved or temporarily null-routed to protect other customers.

## 6. Backups

Backups follow your plan. Download your own world backups before major updates.
