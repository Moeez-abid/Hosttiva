The client area at **client.hosttiva.com** is where you manage everything you buy from Hosttiva.

## The main sections

| Section | What you do there |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | See active services, unpaid invoices and open tickets at a glance |
| **My Services** | Open a hosting account, VPS or app; find login details and change plans |
| **Domains** | Register, renew and transfer domains; manage nameservers and DNS |
| **Billing** | Pay invoices, update your payment method and download receipts |
| **Support** | Open and follow tickets with our team |

## Logging in to your hosting control panel

1. Go to **My Services** and select the service.
2. Use the **Login to cPanel** (or panel) button. It signs you in without a separate password.

## Keep your account secure

- Turn on **two-factor authentication** under your account security settings.
- Add a second contact if someone else manages billing or technical work.
- Never share your client area password in a ticket. Our team will never ask for it.
