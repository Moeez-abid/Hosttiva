## Your accounts

- Turn on **two-factor authentication** in the client area and in WordPress or any app admin.
- Use a password manager and a unique password for every login.
- Remove users and FTP accounts you no longer need.

## Your website

- Keep the CMS, themes and plugins updated; remove the ones you don't use.
- Only install plugins and themes from trusted sources. "Nulled" premium plugins are a common source of malware.
- Keep regular backups, stored off the server, and check that you can restore one.

## Your server (VPS)

- Use SSH keys and disable password logins.
- Keep a firewall that only opens the ports you use.
- Apply operating system updates regularly.

## If you think something is wrong

Open a ticket straight away. Include what you noticed and when. Change passwords from a device you trust.
