## Open webmail

Go to `yourbrand.com/webmail` (or `webmail.yourbrand.com`) and sign in with your full email address and its password. You can also open it from cPanel under **Email Accounts → Check Email**.

## Useful settings

- **Signature:** Settings → Identities. Keep it short; images in signatures often land as attachments.
- **Auto-reply:** set it in cPanel under **Email → Autoresponders**, with start and end dates, so it switches itself off.
- **Filters:** route newsletters or invoices into folders (cPanel **Email Filters**, or the webmail settings).

## Webmail and mail apps together

Webmail, phone and desktop all use the same IMAP mailbox, so read status and folders stay in sync. Deleting a message in one place deletes it everywhere.
