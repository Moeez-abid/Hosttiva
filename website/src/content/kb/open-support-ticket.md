Tickets are the fastest way to reach the Hosttiva team because they are linked to your services.

## Open a ticket

1. Sign in to the client area and choose **Support → Open Ticket**.
2. Pick the department closest to your question (technical, billing or sales).
3. Select the service the ticket is about.

## What to include

- **What you expected** and **what happened instead**.
- The **domain or URL** affected.
- **When it started**, and anything that changed around then (a plugin update, DNS change, new code).
- **Exact error messages**, or a screenshot.
- Steps to reproduce, if you have them.

## What not to include

Passwords, card numbers or other secrets. If we need access, we will explain a safe way to grant it.
