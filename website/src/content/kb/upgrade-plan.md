## Upgrading within the same product line

1. Open **My Services** and select the service.
2. Choose **Upgrade/Downgrade** and pick the new plan.
3. Pay the prorated difference. The new limits apply within minutes; your files, databases and email stay where they are.

## Moving from shared hosting to a VPS or cloud plan

This is a migration rather than an in-place upgrade, because the site moves to a different server:

1. Order the new VPS or managed application plan.
2. Ask our team to migrate the site (free on eligible plans), or follow the [migration guide](/blog/move-website-minimal-downtime).
3. Test the site on the new server, then switch DNS.
4. Cancel the old hosting once you have confirmed everything works.

## Signs it's time to upgrade

- Resource-limit warnings in your control panel during normal traffic.
- Pages slow down at busy times even after caching and image optimisation.
- You need software, background workers or root access that shared hosting doesn't allow.
