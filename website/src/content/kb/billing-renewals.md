## When invoices are created

An invoice is generated a few days before each service renews, so you have time to pay before the due date. You receive it by email and it appears under **Billing → My Invoices**.

## First-term and renewal prices

Many plans have an introductory price for the first term and a renewal price after that. Both are shown on the plan before you order, and the renewal invoice uses the renewal price.

## Auto-renewal

- With a saved payment method, renewals are charged automatically on the due date.
- To stop a service renewing, request cancellation at the end of the billing period from **My Services**.
- Domains renew separately from hosting; check each domain's auto-renew setting under **Domains**.

## Changing your payment method

Go to **Billing → Payment Methods**, add the new method, set it as default, and remove the old one. Card details are stored by our payment provider, not on our servers.

## Missed a payment?

Pay the open invoice from **Billing** as soon as you can. Services that stay unpaid are suspended and later terminated, as set out in the account suspension policy.
