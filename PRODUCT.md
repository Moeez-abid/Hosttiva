# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Balanced audience; no single persona leads the homepage (confirmed). Visitors are small and medium businesses (websites, email, WordPress), developers and startups (VPS, application and AI hosting), agencies (reseller, white-label, custom builds) and gaming communities (game servers). They arrive comparing hosts and want to know what a plan includes, what it costs after the first term, and whether real people will help.

## Product Purpose
Hosttiva by Inventomo sells hosting, domains, business email, cloud/VPS, application deployment, AI and automation hosting, reseller tools and game servers. The public site explains offers, compares plans and routes visitors to the WHMCS client area (client.hosttiva.com) to order, or to an enquiry for quote-only work. Success: a visitor finds the right product line and either orders or enquires.

## Positioning
Hosttiva is the hosting and cloud division of Inventomo, a development company. "Hosttiva hosts your technology. Inventomo helps you create it." Infrastructure plus the people who can design, build, migrate and support what runs on it.

## Operating Context
Ordering, billing, tickets and domains live in a separate WHMCS + Lagom client area; the site never fakes checkout. Plan buttons go to WHMCS; quote items go to the WHMCS contact form. Free tools (DNS, WHOIS, domain generator, SSL) run on the site.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Astro static site on Cloudflare Pages; data-driven pages from `website/src/data/*.ts`.
- Prices, marketplace delivery times and support periods are placeholders and must be shown as such.
- Conditional products (GPU, Windows, AI website builder, agency directory) are "Coming soon" only; no affiliate commission %, no Envato names.
- Legal, infrastructure, uptime and compliance facts are not yet confirmed and must not be invented.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: "Hosttiva by Inventomo". Tagline: "Host what you build." "Powered by Inventomo" in the footer.
- Personality: professional, modern, developer-friendly, honest, reliable, human support, AI-forward. Voice: simple language, no exaggerated claims, transparent pricing.
- Visual direction pinned by the user (2026-10-20): Apple design language with tasteful glass; Apple system blue as the single accent; theme follows the visitor's OS setting with a manual toggle. Avoid AI-slop patterns.

## Evidence on Hand
No logo files, product screenshots, photos, testimonials, customer logos, benchmarks or uptime data. Visuals must be authored (illustrative product UI) and labelled as illustrative where a visitor could mistake them for real data. Do not fabricate customers, reviews, data centres or metrics.

## Product Principles
1. Show the price you will pay next year, not just this year.
2. Only publish what Hosttiva can deliver today; everything else is visibly "coming soon".
3. One account, one team: hosting and development are a single story.
4. Plain language first, specs one click away.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA (SRS §6): contrast, keyboard navigation, focus states, semantic HTML, reduced motion respected.
